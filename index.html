CloudSense Admin Console

User Manual & Feature Reference Guide

Dashboard Overview

The top four metric cards give you a real-time snapshot of your cloud infrastructure. Active Instances shows how many servers are currently running. Recommended Shutdown flags instances whose CPU is below 15% — a reliable signal of waste. Current Monthly Cost is the live spend across all running instances. Potential Savings is the dollar difference between your current spend and what you would pay after applying all recommendations.

Charts & Visualizations

Average Team CPU Utilization

A bar chart comparing CPU usage across all teams. Teams with very low bars are prime candidates for resource consolidation.

Resource Heatmap

A horizontal stacked bar showing individual employee CPU usage grouped by team. Useful for spotting which team has the highest workload density.

Cost Forecast

Projects your monthly spend over the next 5 months. The Optimized Path line shows your projected cost if all idle instances are shut down today.

Admin Console Controls

Infrastructure Simulation Slider

Drag the slider to simulate running fewer instances. The dashboard updates in real-time to show the cost and efficiency impact of any hypothetical configuration.

Reset Environment

Restores all instances to their default active state and clears any CSV data you may have uploaded.

Upload Real Data (CSV)

Replace the demo data with your own. The CSV must have columns: team, employee, instance_type, monthly_cost, cpu_usage, ram_usage. Download the template for the exact format.

Export Executive Report

Downloads a CSV summary of all currently active instances, their costs, and usage — ready for a board presentation or finance review.

Optimization & Auto-Pilot

Auto-Optimize

A one-click action that immediately suspends all instances flagged as idle (CPU < 15%). Run this to instantly capture your maximum projected savings.

Autonomous Auto-Pilot

When enabled, the system monitors CPU usage every 4.5 seconds and automatically suspends any newly-idle instance. A toast notification confirms each action taken.

Team & Instance Management

The Management tab lets you control resources at two levels. Use Team Cards to stop or start all instances for an entire team at once. Use the Employee List to suspend or resume individual instances. All changes reflect immediately across all charts and metrics.

Portal Access

CloudSense supports two portals. Admin Console provides full visibility and control over all teams and instances. Employee Portal gives each employee a personal, read-only view of their own server metrics and team overview. Employees authenticate via Google SSO and then enter a personal PIN to access their data.
